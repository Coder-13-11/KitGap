# KitGap launch video: shot list (about 2:45)

Built like a product trailer: a personal reason, the problem in one line, then the product doing real work on screen, then the mission. Everything shown is the real app in the demo. The places and people in the demo are made up and say "(sample)"; say so once.

**Rules for the challenge:** 1 to 3 minutes. Say that AI helped build it and that Kit uses AI. Don't claim real users, partners or donations that haven't happened.

## Before you record

- Use Chrome on your Mac. Close other tabs, turn on Do Not Disturb, and set browser zoom to 110–125%.
- Open `http://localhost:3000/demo` (or the deployed site). Click **Reset the demo** right before recording.
- Test the microphone once: open Kit, tap the mic, allow it, say "What should I do next?".
- Record with QuickTime (File → New Screen Recording) or OBS. For phone shots, use the browser's phone size, or iPhone Mirroring.
- Saves take a few seconds locally. Cut the waits in editing (iMovie or CapCut).
- Record your voice separately if you can. It's easier to re-do one line.

## Lines you can borrow (say them your way)

- "Proof, not promises."
- "Bring exactly what's needed, and see it counted."
- "Kit doesn't guess. Every number comes from the need itself."

## If something breaks while recording

- **Kit is slow or says something odd:** ask again, or tap one of the suggested questions. If the AI is down, Kit still answers from its written rules.
- **The demo looks wrong:** click Reset the demo.
- **The mic doesn't work:** check Chrome's site settings (the lock icon in the address bar), then reload.
